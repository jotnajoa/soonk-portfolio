## 1. Grid Strategy

Recommended: **4 × 4 grid (16 tiles)** — fills cleanly.
Alternative: **3 × 3 (9 tiles)** if more aggressive editing wanted (drop personal experiments + merge publications). Notes at bottom.

Tile typology — three styles of main keyword:
- **Quoted phrase tiles** — methodology / move (e.g. "FLIPPED THE ASK")
- **Single-word verbs / nouns** — capability (e.g. CUT THE RITUAL, NON-NEGOTIABLE)
- **Category labels** — credibility (e.g. PARSONS, AI UX)

Mixing the three is intentional. Quoted tiles act as visual anchors; the rest support.

Row narrative for the 4×4:

| Row | Theme | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client / enterprise | Senior operator + leadership signature |
| 2 | Solo founder work | 0→1 product + GTM execution |
| 3 | Personal experiments | "Keeps building" — curiosity |
| 4 | Publications & teaching | Thought leadership / credibility |

#### 01. GIA — Deloitte

- **Supporting:** *velocity > process · enterprise scale*
- **Subtitle:** GIA · Deloitte's enterprise platform

- **Body bullets:**

  - Cut UX rituals that no longer earned their place; minimized the design system to unlock team velocity
  - Led parallel offshore + onshore design teams across time zones
  - Diagnosed the real bottleneck as velocity, not quality, and stripped accordingly

#### 02. Alnylam SSOT
- **Main keyword:** `"WRONG ON PURPOSE"`
- **Supporting:** *fixed scope · earned the expansion*
- **Subtitle:** Single Source of Truth — high-stakes platform pivot
- **Role:** Operator / strategist · war-room mode
- **Lens:** Operator under constraint
- **Hero copy:** Sketches as catalysts, not artifacts. The wrong one made the right one obvious.
- **Body bullets:**
  - Two-month fixed timeline + fixed budget. No room for traditional UX process.
  - Drew flawed sketches on purpose to provoke reaction — exposed misalignment faster than open discussion ever could
  - Worked directly with business partners in real-time war-room mode; sketch → review → refine → forward, all day
  - Earned client confidence sufficient to expand the engagement

#### 03. Toyota Guide Hub
- **Main keyword:** `NON-NEGOTIABLE`
- **Supporting:** *senior team · principled critique*
- **Subtitle:** Supply chain operations platform
- **Role:** Senior designer / acting design lead — emergency engagement
- **Lens:** Design manager
- **Hero copy:** Couldn't critique opinion. So I anchored every fix in undebatable principle.
- **Body bullets:**
  - Parachuted into a mid-to-senior team that had lost momentum, design fundamentals, and stakeholder trust
  - Anchored every critique in objectively-broken patterns — action clarity (button priority), color semantics mapping, viewport reality (Figma canvas vs. implementation) — so the conversation became professional, not personal
  - Trimmed UX bloat — process not grounded in impact
  - Restored team velocity and stakeholder alignment

#### 04. Advanced Reporting — Teachable
- **Main keyword:** `"LOW USAGE ≠ LOW VALUE"`
- **Supporting:** *scoped persona · 87% engagement*
- **Subtitle:** Creator analytics · Teachable
- **Role:** Solo IC designer, end-to-end (discovery → ship → measure)
- **Lens:** Senior IC
- **Hero copy:** I refused to read low engagement as failure. The data was telling a different story.
- **Body bullets:**
  - Segmented users by data literacy and explicitly cut the power-user persona from V1 — chose Traffic & Conversion at the school level because it was the only metric set applicable to all audiences
  - Specified Bubble + Line chart combo to handle the value-range mismatch between traffic and conversion
  - Designed measurable success criteria upfront (engagement score + NPS); instrumented alpha with Fullstory + Pendo + Sigma
  - Shipped to 87% engagement vs. previous 20%; reframed event-driven usage instead of reading low visits as failure

### Row 2 — Solo Founder (Product + GTM split)

---

#### 05A. POMEs — Product
- **Main keyword:** `"FLIPPED THE ASK"`
- **Supporting:** *behavior > brief*
- **Subtitle:** POMEs · neighborhood karma app
- **Role:** Solo founder — research, design, build
- **Lens:** Product person
- **Hero copy:** Users said one thing. Their behavior said another. I built for the behavior.
- **Body bullets:**
  - Observed via WhatsApp behavioral data that people don't post "Help me with X" — the social cost of asking is too high
  - Reframed the core action: **"I can help with X."** Posting becomes a status gain, not a status cost
  - Reframed Seeds (the karma system) from a transactional scoreboard into a "community balance device" — hid the score
  - US Provisional Patent in flight

---

#### 05B. POMEs — GTM
- **Main keyword:** `ELEVATOR ONLY`
- **Supporting:** *distribution ceiling · NYC ground game*
- **Subtitle:** POMEs · go-to-market
- **Role:** Solo founder — field marketing
- **Lens:** Distribution / growth
- **Hero copy:** Tested 4 channels in NYC rental buildings. One worked. The lesson was bigger than the channel.
- **Body bullets:**
  - Falsified the first hypothesis (owners care most) in market — owners already knew their neighbors and had no resource scarcity. Pivoted ICP to **renters**: new to building, thin network, sharing economy makes financial sense
  - Mapped NYC rental neighborhoods (Greenpoint · LIC · Williamsburg · DUMBO) by building type — Managed / Hybrid / Open — to define penetration strategy
  - A/B tested 4 distribution channels: elevator, lobby drop, mailroom/amenity, local café partnerships
  - **Elevator was explosive. Others negligible.** 2-week return to same building → no incremental engagement
  - Surfaced principle: **residential adoption has a ceiling.** Additional exposure does not buy additional participation

## 4. 3×3 alternative (compressed)

If the 4×4 feels too dense, drop to 9 tiles:

```
[ 01 CUT THE RITUAL  ] [ 02 "WRONG ON PURPOSE"  ] [ 03 NON-NEGOTIABLE       ]
[ 04 "LOW USAGE ≠..."] [ POMEs (combined)        ] [ VoltHop (combined)      ]
[ EXPERIMENTS (idx)  ] [ WRITING (idx)           ] [ TEACHING (idx)          ]
```

Tradeoffs:
- Loses the product/GTM split for POMEs and VoltHop (the strongest narrative beat in the portfolio).
- Loses tile-level granularity for personal experiments and publications.
- Bigger tiles = each carries more weight, more typographic real estate.

If going 3×3, keep the product/GTM story inside the combined tile via internal anchors.

## 5. Tone notes for the build

- **Hero copy** is single-line, first-person, declarative. No hedging.
- **Body bullets** are short, evidence-led. Numbers when available.
- **Quote-style keywords** (in `"..."`) act as visual anchors — typeset them differently from single-word keywords.
- Avoid the standard "Role / Tools / Year" trio — use only what earns its place.
- Project links should feel like footnotes, not CTAs.
